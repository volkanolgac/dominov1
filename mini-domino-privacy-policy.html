Privacy Policy for Mini Domino

Effective date: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mini Domino handles information when you use the game. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

Mini Domino is designed to be a simple game. We do not intentionally collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, postal address, or telephone number through the game itself.

2. Device and Usage Information

Depending on the version of the game and the services enabled on your device, third-party platforms such as Google Play Services, advertising providers, analytics providers, or crash-reporting services may automatically process limited technical information. This may include device information, approximate usage information, diagnostics, and crash data.

Such information is processed by the relevant third-party service providers under their own privacy policies. Mini Domino does not use this information to personally identify you.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

If advertising or other third-party services are enabled in a version of Mini Domino, those services may collect or process information in accordance with their own privacy policies. The use of such services may include advertising identifiers or similar technical identifiers where permitted by your device and applicable law.

Third-party services may change over time. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of the relevant service providers for additional information about their practices.

4. Children's Privacy

Mini Domino does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us at the email address below so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled in connection with the game. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We do not maintain a database of users' personal information through Mini Domino. Information that may be processed by third-party services is retained according to the policies and retention practices of those providers.

7. Your Choices

You can manage certain privacy and advertising settings through your Android device and Google account. You may also limit or reset your advertising identifier through your device settings, where available.
